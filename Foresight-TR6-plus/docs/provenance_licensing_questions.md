# Provenance, Licensing and Redistribution Questions

Status: unresolved; publication and redistribution are not authorized by this workspace.

## Source provenance

The extension references the local TR-6 files and reuses the completed read-only audit. It does not claim authorship of TR-6 or alter source labels.

Required answers from the source dataset owner:

1. Who collected TR-6, at which institution and under which protocol?
2. What do `Normal`, `Classified`, `Spoiled` and `Not_spoiled` formally mean?
3. Who assigned spoilage labels, at what time and using which observable or laboratory criterion?
4. Is `Classified` intended as a derived partition of `Normal`?
5. Why do 170 exact image-content groups occur under both spoilage labels?
6. How many physical specimens exist, and what does methane `case N` mean?
7. What do methane `R` and `L` mean?
8. Which image dates correspond to methane `Day N`?
9. How were the 17 views ordered, and which view is top-down?
10. Were RGB, thermal and methane clocks synchronized?
11. What do the two Celsius-like values in thermal filenames represent?
12. Are thermal JPEGs radiometric measurements, rendered pseudocolor images, fusion renderings or another product?

## Licensing

Before IEEE DataPort submission, obtain and archive:

- The original TR-6 license and redistribution terms.
- Written permission to publish derived manifests, thumbnails and annotations.
- Attribution requirements and a preferred citation.
- Confirmation that filenames and metadata contain no restricted information.
- Any institutional, ethics or data-use approvals applicable to acquisition.

No license file is created here because choosing a license without authority would be misleading.

## Redistribution design

Until rights are confirmed:

- Keep source images outside the extension workspace.
- Publish neither images nor source methane text.
- Treat contact sheets as local review artifacts only.
- Do not upload manifests containing local absolute paths.
- For a future release, prefer relative paths plus source checksums and provide images only if redistribution is explicitly permitted.

## Publication blockers

The extension is not IEEE DataPort-ready until source rights, label provenance, conflict adjudication, annotation completion, quality-control results, data dictionary review and official leakage-safe splits are documented.
